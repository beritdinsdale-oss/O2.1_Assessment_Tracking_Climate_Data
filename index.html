Module 2 · Widen the view

What changes when we look across many years?

In Module 1, one growing season helped us separate weather from climate. Now we’ll use a longer record to look for a climate pattern.

You’ll start with the 2024 weather you already know, then widen the view to decades of temperature observations from the same Corvallis State University station.

Long-term data: NOAA/NCEI Global Summary of the Year, Corvallis State University station (USC00351862). Years with no reported annual mean temperature are left out.

The lens is wider now

One year is weather. Many years reveal climate.

ONE YEARWeather varies

A year may be warmer, cooler, wetter, or drier than normal.

MANY YEARSPatterns emerge

Climate trends become visible when we examine observations across decades.

The Corvallis record is a good reminder that year-to-year variability and a long-term trend can exist at the same time.
