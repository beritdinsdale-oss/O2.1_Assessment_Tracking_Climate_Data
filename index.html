Weather and Climate: What Does the Corvallis Graph Show?

Learn how to read a weather-station graph, practice distinguishing weather from climate, and then explore a station yourself.

Learning goal

Explain how temperatures can vary from year to year while still showing a long-term climate pattern.

1. Watch: How to explore a weather station

Watch the demonstration. You will use the same process yourself in Step 3.

3. Try it yourself with another weather station

Now use the process demonstrated in the video to explore a station near you—or another place you are interested in.

  1. Open xmACIS in a new tab.
  2. Choose a single-station temperature graph.
  3. Search for and select a weather station.
  4. Choose a time period that covers many years.
  5. Look for short-term ups and downs as well as the longer-term pattern.
