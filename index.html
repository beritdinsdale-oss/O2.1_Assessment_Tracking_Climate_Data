A climate story in data

What can one weather station tell us?

Weather stations record conditions year after year. Looking across many years lets us move beyond individual weather events and start seeing climate patterns.

📅 One year

Shows variability and unusual weather.

📈 Many years

Reveal longer-term climate patterns.
